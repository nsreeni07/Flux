# May 27: Cad and Reasearch
So I wanted to make a fume exttractor today, I first worked on the cad adn went with a 2 fan stacked on each other layout, I also added fume hoods which can be folded in for easy storage, I did this because I'm going to be doing a lot of soldering over the summer and the last thing I need is to inhale a bunch of flux and solder fumes. 
I picked 2 120mm computer fans and a flexible duct to route the fumes out my window, I already have a carbon filter which I will add into the build. I'm also going to have a led light strip at the top to help for visibility when soldering. 
I ran into some issue with the CAD where edges I wanted filleted wouldnt fillet but I found a workaround, 
I went on aliexpress and made a BOM which came out to close to $30 in total. I also did some work off the time lapse  working on the repo
